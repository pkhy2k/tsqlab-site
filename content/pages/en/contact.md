Tourism Service Quality Lab accepts the following inquiries by email. English and Korean are both welcome.

- **Contributions and interviews**: requests for articles or interviews from media outlets, academic societies and institutions. Please include the outlet, topic, format and preferred schedule.
- **Research partnerships**: proposals for joint research, data collaboration or academic events. Please include your organization, the research topic and the expected timeline.
- **Corrections and feedback**: factual errors or comments on published articles. Please include the article title or URL and the part concerned.
