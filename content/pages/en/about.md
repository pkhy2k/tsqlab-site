## The Lab

Tourism Service Quality Lab (관광서비스품질연구소) is a non-profit academic research group based in Korea. We study tourism policy, trends in the hotel and resort industry, service quality in tourism and hospitality, and hotel IT solutions. Our aim is to connect service quality theory with frontline hotel operations and hotel IT practice, and to draw implications that matter to both academia and industry.

We publish our work as articles and columns on this website. The Lab is not a registered business and does not operate for profit.

## Research fields

- **Policy & Trends**: We read tourism policy and industry trends through the lens of service quality.
- **Hotels & Resorts**: We follow market trends and business strategies in hotels and resorts in Korea and abroad.
- **Service Quality**: We cover the theory, measurement and improvement of service quality in tourism and hospitality.
- **Hotel IT**: We study how hotel IT solutions are adopted and run, and how they shape guest experience.

## Editorial principles

Some articles are published under the name of the Lab's editorial team, and AI tools are used in drafting and translation. Editorial articles are published after the cited sources and facts are checked, and the Director reviews each article after publication. Errors are corrected promptly once confirmed. Articles submitted by members are published under the author's name after review by the editorial committee and approval by the Director.

English pages are translations of the Korean originals. Where the two differ, the Korean original prevails. Not every article is available in English yet.

## Organization

The Lab has a research committee and an editorial committee under the Director, and an advisory group consisting of senior advisors and an advisory committee. The research committee conducts research and the editorial committee reviews manuscripts for publication. Senior advisors and advisory committee members are appointed by the Director and advise on research directions and industry issues independently of the Lab's operations.
